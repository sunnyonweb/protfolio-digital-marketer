## 🇮🇳 Indian Client – High-Impact Google Ads Campaign

Over a **14-day period**, I executed a results-driven **Google Ads campaign** for an Indian client, generating **2,120 highly relevant clicks** at an **average CPC of just ₹2 (~$0.02)**, with a **total spend of only ₹3,600 (~$42.97)**.  
The campaign also achieved **359,000 impressions**, significantly boosting brand visibility and driving **targeted traffic** to the client’s website.

This success was achieved through **strategic keyword targeting**, **compelling ad copy**, and **precision bid management**, transforming a modest budget into a **high-performance traffic engine**.  
The project demonstrates my ability to **maximize ROI**, deliver **measurable results**, and manage **cost-efficient PPC campaigns** tailored to the Indian market.
