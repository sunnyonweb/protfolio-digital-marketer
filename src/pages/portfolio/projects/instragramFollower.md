**Instagram Follower Growth Campaign — Cost-Effective Results** showcases successful targeted campaigns for a US-based clothing brand using Meta Ads Manager and Instagram Ads.

With a budget of **$108**, the campaigns reached **141 highly-targeted prospects**, generating meaningful conversations at an exceptionally low cost per message.

The Instagram follower/profile visit campaign achieved **1,292 profile visits** at a cost per result of just **$0.05**, with a total spend of **$69.85**.

Through **strategic optimization**, **precise audience targeting**, and **real-time data analysis**, these campaigns maximized engagement and ROI. Several campaigns were recognized as **“High Performing”** or **“Recommended”**, highlighting expertise in budget allocation, bidding strategies, and performance-driven advertising.
