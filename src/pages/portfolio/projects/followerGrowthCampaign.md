**Follower Growth Campaign — Cost-Effective Results** demonstrates highly cost-effective audience growth using Meta Ads Manager.

Through **strategic targeting** and **continuous optimization**, the campaign drove rapid follower growth while maximizing ROI, showcasing expertise in social media campaign management and data-driven decision-making.
